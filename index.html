The candidate (fictional): Rudi Rentier

Rudi Rentier is invented for this demo. The profile, the roles, the companies and every score on the job list are made up; any likeness to a real person, company or advert is accidental.

  • Nine years in product, the last three on AI features: first the search and recommendation team of a consumer travel app, then the features built on language models for its customers.
  • Before product, three years as a mobile developer.
  • Strong at: understanding what users want and need, outstandingly so; customer experience, for example service journeys and support that users rate well; turning what a model can do into a feature people keep using; running experiments; working closely with data scientists and designers.
  • Looking for: a senior or lead product role on AI products, permanent, at the North Pole, in English or German; willing to relocate to the South Pole; not willing to relocate to a city with a hot climate.
  • Not looking for: pure delivery or project management, agencies and consultancies, ad tech, gambling.
  • Languages: English and German fluent, Spanish conversational.

How Job Scout scores a role for this candidate, each from 1 to 10:

  • cap: how much of the role the candidate has already done;
  • sen: whether the level fits nine years of experience;
  • nar: how well the role continues the candidate's story (AI features, customer experience, consumer);
  • int: how likely an application is to get to an interview.

A role is pursue, borderline or skip from these and the hard filters (location, level, the kinds of company above).
